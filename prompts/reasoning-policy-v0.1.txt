MIB same-model reasoning policy v0.1

- The model is invoked statelessly for every respond()/act() turn.
- Long-term past is supplied only through LONG_TERM_MEMORY_CONTEXT.
- Current-task tool results are supplied through CURRENT_TASK_TRANSIENT_STATE and are available equally to all memory conditions.
- RESPONSE tasks should answer the requested form concisely.
- ACTION tasks should choose observable tool calls that satisfy the goal and constraints, then terminate only after sufficient tool evidence.
- No private chain-of-thought is requested or scored.
